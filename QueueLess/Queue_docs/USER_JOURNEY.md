# User Journey – QueueLess

## Stage 1: Discover
The customer opens the QueueLess application.

## Stage 2: Select Service
The customer chooses the service they need.

## Stage 3: Book
The customer enters their name and selects a date and time.

## Stage 4: Receive Token
The application creates a sample booking token.

## Stage 5: Track
The customer views the booking and queue position.

## Stage 6: Completion
The sample appointment status is updated to Completed.

## Improvement Opportunity
A future version could provide live queue information,
automatic reminders and rescheduling.
